You are a developer who needs databases, a key-value store and object storage for an application on Deploio, and you want to understand the options before you choose.

Create an Economy and a Business database as the configuring your database guide shows, connect to them as it shows, and then set up the key-value store and object storage from the other dependencies guide.
No application is needed. Where a guide wants to bind a service to an application, deploy the example Go application from `{{docs}}/go/quick-start.md` with a name of your own and use it.
You are done when you have worked through both guides, or when you are stuck.
